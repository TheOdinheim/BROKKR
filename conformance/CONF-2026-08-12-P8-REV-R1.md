# Conformance Check CONF-2026-08-12-P8-REV-R1

**Revision:** `brokkr-sentinel` + `brokkr-audit` Rev 1.12 adoption — **pre-build Step-0 audit**
**Subject:** the committed `brokkr-core` Rev 1.12 surface (`ResolutionDecision.{nonce,expiry}`,
`resolution_signed_content`, `ToleranceError::SignatureInvalid`, `ResolveError`) audited for whether the
sentinel+audit adoption can enforce what it places
**Date:** 12 August 2026
**Method (CLAUDE.md §5.3):** requirements enumerated **from the corpus** (AMD-005 OQGF-P-8.2/P-8.5; AMD-002
OQGF-P-4; AMD-001 OQGF-M-14 as it bears on freshness; AMD-007 OQGF-I-13 for the audit record), quoted, never
from BROKKR-ARCH §14. Because the build **stopped at Step 0** (GAP-2026-08-12-001), no requirement is
`satisfied` by running code; each is marked *buildable-not-built*, *blocked*, or *traced*.

**What was checked, and against what (§7):** the freshness-enforcement, forged-grant, and audit-record
obligations of Rev 1.12, against the committed core surface and the sentinel/audit code that would adopt it.
Finding: **one blocker** — `ResolveError` cannot express a freshness/replay refusal — plus the buildable
remainder.

---

## 1. AMD-005 — OQGF-P-8.5 (fail-safe asymmetry + freshness)

> *"…SHALL NOT autonomously de-escalate above Baseline… Where uncertain, it SHALL remain escalated."* +
> §6.8 (Rev 1.12): *"EIR SHALL refuse a decision whose `now > expiry`, and SHALL refuse a `nonce` it has
> already accepted for that escalation."*

| Aspect | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Verify signature under the DAP key over **core's** encoding (issuer/verifier agree) | **buildable-not-built** | `resolve` would call `brokkr_core::resolution::resolution_signed_content`; Task 1 |
| **Refuse `now > expiry`** | **BLOCKED** | `resolve` returns `Result<_, ResolveError>`; no variant means "expired" (GAP-2026-08-12-001) |
| **Refuse a replayed `nonce`** | **BLOCKED** | as above; no variant means "replayed" |

**Verdict: still PARTIAL — NOT moved to satisfied.** The core revision placed the type surface; this
revision was to make it *enforced*, and cannot: the refusal has no honest `ResolveError` to return.
Enforcement waits on the core `ResolveError` variant (the gap's recommendation). **Silence here would be a
false pass** — the fields exist and are signed over, but nothing refuses an expired or replayed decision.

## 2. AMD-005 — OQGF-P-8.2 (resolution is an act)

**Verdict: traced — unchanged.** `nonce`/`expiry` are freshness bounds, not a timeout to baseline; nothing
resolves because a timer expired. Adoption does not change this.

## 3. AMD-002 — OQGF-P-4 (tolerance grant signed)

> *"…PQC-signed… Expired or out-of-scope grants SHALL have no effect."*

**Verdict: buildable-not-built.** `ToleranceError::SignatureInvalid` exists (Rev 1.12); `grant_heuristic`'s
first (signature) check would return it instead of `OutOfScope`, so a forged grant is reported **as forged**
— one true, first-failing reason (§6.7). Not built because the revision stopped as a whole (§5 of the
report); it is not itself blocked.

## 4. AMD-001 — OQGF-M-14 (freshness pattern) as it bears

**Verdict: traced.** M-14's nonce+expiry pattern now applies to the resolution decision (placed by the core
revision). Enforcing it here is the blocked half.

## 5. AMD-007 — OQGF-I-13 (Barrier decisions recorded in Organ 5)

> *"Every Barrier decision … SHALL be recorded in Organ 5 …"* — and, for a resolution event, the audit
> record SHALL faithfully encode the decision it records.

**Verdict: buildable-not-built.** `brokkr-audit`'s `write_resolution` omits `nonce`/`expiry`, so two
decisions differing only in freshness would encode identically in the audit record — the audit spine holding
an incomplete account of the one act that lowers a defense. The fix (append `d.nonce.0`, `d.expiry.0` in
field order) is buildable; the audit **record** domain tag (`brokkr-audit:record:v1`) is unchanged, so
four-crate domain separation is unaffected. Not built because the revision stopped as a whole.

## 7. Result

**Not a clean pass, and not asserted as one.** OQGF-P-8.5 **remains PARTIAL** — its freshness enforcement is
**BLOCKED** behind GAP-2026-08-12-001 (`ResolveError` has no expired/replayed variant), so this revision
does **not** move it to satisfied. OQGF-P-4 (`SignatureInvalid`) and OQGF-I-13 (audit nonce/expiry) are
**buildable-not-built**; OQGF-P-8.2 and M-14 **traced**. No verdict moved backward. Zero requirements are
`satisfied` because nothing was built — the build stopped at Step 0 on a missing-variant gap that a
`brokkr-core` revision must place before the sentinel+audit adoption can enforce what Rev 1.12 placed.
