**Author:** Claude Code (builder).

## What was checked, and against what

The committed `brokkr-core` KVASIR **type surface** (`adapt.rs`, and `ScreenPass` in `tolerance.rs`)
after this revision, checked against the text of AMD-003's normative requirements and against the
Rev 1.17 §6.12 placement that governs them. This revision is **types only** — it places no gate
logic. Where §6.12 says a gate *evaluates* something at Phase 9, the correct verdict here is that the
**type surface** is present or partial, and enforcement is Phase 9; a requirement is **not** claimed
satisfied on the strength of a placed type. The prompt is explicit on P-6.2/P-6.3.

Per CLAUDE.md §5.3, a verdict may move backward. KVASIR has never been built (Phase 9), so these
requirements carry no prior BUILT verdict to move; this is their first enumeration at the type-surface
level. No verdict moved.

## OQGF-P-6.1 — Incident-Seeded Refinement

> "A Refined Detector MAY be generated only from a Seeding Incident — a DAP-confirmed true positive
> recorded in Organ 5. Unconfirmed, auto-labeled, or heuristically-scored detections SHALL NOT seed
> adaptation."

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Implementation | `adapt::SeedingIncident { incident_id, confirmed_by: Dap, attack_class }`; `MaturationPipeline::generate(&SeedingIncident) -> Result<RefinedDetector, AdaptError>` with `AdaptError::UnconfirmedSeed`. Unchanged by this revision. |
| Proof | `tests/negative_invariants.rs::test_i9_*` exercises `generate`; the `confirmed_by: Dap` field is required by type (an unconfirmed seed is unconstructable). |
| Verdict | **type surface present; enforcement Phase 9.** The refusal of unconfirmed seeds is the implementor's (`generate`); no `brokkr-core` change here. Not claimed satisfied. |

## OQGF-P-6.2 — Selection on Independent Evidence

> "A Refined Detector SHALL be activated only if it demonstrates improved detection of the Seeding
> Incident's attack class against an Evaluation Corpus that is independent of the seeding sample. A
> candidate that improves only on the single seeding sample, or that improves detection at the cost
> of degraded coverage elsewhere in the corpus, SHALL be disqualified."

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Implementation | `SelectionPass { version: CorpusVersion, produced_at: Timestamp }` (this revision adds `produced_at`); `select(&RefinedDetector, &EvaluationCorpus) -> Result<SelectionPass, AdaptError>` with `AdaptError::{Overfit, CoverageRegression}`. **The `EvaluationCorpusContent` seam §6.12 specifies is NOT placed** — it references `Observation` (a `brokkr-sentinel` type), and core SHALL NOT depend on sentinel (I-5); it is a Phase-9 `brokkr-adapt` crate-local type. |
| Proof | `test_i9_*` constructs a `SelectionPass`; no measurement is exercised (Phase 9). |
| Verdict | **partial (type surface).** `SelectionPass` now version-checkable; the corpus-content seam is deliberately absent from core and the measurement gate is Phase 9. **Not claimed satisfied** (per prompt). |

## OQGF-P-6.3 — Tolerance-Gated Activation

> "No Refined Detector SHALL activate until it passes central-tolerance screening (OQGF-P-3) against
> the current Self Set. A refinement that improves detection but raises host harm above the declared
> bound SHALL be discarded regardless of its detection gains."

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Implementation | `tolerance::ScreenPass { version: SelfSetVersion, produced_at: Timestamp }` (this revision adds `produced_at`); carried into `DetectorProvenance::screen_result`; `activate` gains `now: Timestamp` (I-13). The **version-currency gate** (refuse a pass whose version ≠ current) and the tolerance screen itself are Phase-9/Phase-8 logic, not placed here. |
| Proof | `test_i9_*` shows `activate` returns `Err(FailsTolerance)` from a mock; no real screen runs (Phase 9). |
| Verdict | **partial (type surface).** `ScreenPass` now carries the version it screened against + a produced-at audit stamp, and `activate` receives `now`; the gate that evaluates currency is Phase 9. **Not claimed satisfied** (per prompt). |

## OQGF-P-6.4 — Detector Provenance

> "Every Refined Detector SHALL carry signed Detector Provenance — the Seeding Incident identifier,
> the Evaluation Corpus version, the self-tolerance screening result, and the approving DAP —
> recorded in Organ 5. A detector whose provenance cannot be reconstructed SHALL NOT be active."

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Implementation | `DetectorProvenance { seeding: IncidentId, corpus_version: CorpusVersion, screen_result: ScreenPass, approver: Dap, signature: DualSignature }`, and it is a **required parameter** of `activate`. Structural: activation without provenance is uncallable. **This revision does not change `DetectorProvenance`** (its `screen_result` now transitively carries `produced_at`). |
| Proof | `provenance()` in `tests/negative_invariants.rs` constructs the full record; `activate` takes it by value. |
| Verdict | **satisfied at the type level; enforcement (Organ-5 recording) Phase 9.** Provenance is a precondition of activation by construction, not a runtime check. |

## OQGF-P-6.5 — Reversibility

> "Every activated Refined Detector SHALL be versioned and reversible. A rollback SHALL be a recorded
> event in Organ 5 carrying its justification and the acting DAP."

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Implementation | `RefinedDetector` carries a private `generation: u64`; `activate` **returns** `PriorGeneration`, so an activation that does not surrender a handle to what it replaced is unrepresentable. Unchanged by this revision. |
| Proof | `MockPipeline::activate` returns `Result<PriorGeneration, _>`; the return type is the guarantee. |
| Verdict | **satisfied at the type level; enforcement (recorded rollback) Phase 9.** |

## OQGF-P-6.6 — No Autonomous Activation above Baseline

> "At Enhanced assurance and above, activation of a Refined Detector SHALL require DAP approval.
> Autonomous generation and autonomous selection are permitted; autonomous activation is not."

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Implementation | `AdaptError::NeedsApproval`; `DetectorProvenance::approver: Dap` (an activation names an approving DAP by type). DAP approval is enforced in the Phase-9 implementor of `activate`. |
| Proof | `test_i9_*` asserts `activate` returns `Err(NeedsApproval)` without approval (mock). |
| Verdict | **type surface present; enforcement (require DAP approval) Phase 9.** Traced. |

## Structural invariants

### I-9 — a RefinedDetector is Heuristic by construction; nothing learned may touch a deterministic gate

`RefinedDetector`'s fields are private; `new()` fixes `response_class` to `ResponseClass::Heuristic`;
no field/setter/conversion yields `Deterministic`. **Confirmed untouched** by this revision (the diff
does not touch `RefinedDetector` or its `impl`). `brokkr-adapt` does not exist and will not depend on
`brokkr-reasoner` (I-9's dependency clause is a Phase-9 crate fact, not a core one).

**Verdict: satisfied (structural, unchanged).**

### I-13 — a gate that evaluates an expiry takes `now` as a parameter of the evaluating call

`MaturationPipeline::activate` now takes `now: Timestamp` per call — it is the method that evaluates
a pass against the present (§6.12). `generate` and `select` evaluate no time and take none. No clock
is held in construction state in the touched surface. §6.12's rule is documented on the passes and on
`activate`: **version equality is the gate; `now` is the audit trail.**

**Verdict: satisfied at the type level for the KVASIR surface** (per-call `now` on the one
time-evaluating method; enforcement of the version-currency check is Phase 9).

## Findings

**Zero requirements ABSENT.** No §4 gap; the build is not stopped.

The check states plainly what it examined (the committed KVASIR type surface) and against what
(AMD-003 §AMD.1 quoted). A zero-ABSENT result here is not a coverage claim: every P-6.x above is
recorded as *type surface / enforcement Phase 9*, not as satisfied, because this revision places types
and not gates.

**One cross-scope consequence, not a conformance finding:** adding `produced_at` to `ScreenPass`
breaks `brokkr-sentinel/heimdall.rs:406` (co-owned type). It is a mechanical ripple requiring a
companion Phase-8 revision, reported in PHASE-1-REV-2026-08-19-R11.md §4, not a framework gap.
