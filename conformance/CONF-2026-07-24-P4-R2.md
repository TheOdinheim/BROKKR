## 2. OQGF-M-8 — what this phase closes, and the residual

**This phase closes the chain-verification-in-the-gate half of M-8.** SINDRI verifies a chain
to its root using **only** resolver-supplied public roots of trust (`DualPublicKey`), holding
no private material — the gate never has a signer's keypair. The standing verdict in
`CONF-2026-07-16-P3-R1` was **partial** ("SINDRI must be wired to resolve attestation-sourced
keys and call `verify_chain_public`"); **Phase 4 executes exactly that wiring**, so the gate
half is now satisfied.

**The residual (Option B, named per §6.4.1 and ARCH §13):** the roots of trust are a
**declared registry** — `RegistryResolver` maps a `SubjectId` to declared public-key bytes,
supplied out of band. Trust in a hop's key therefore rests on **pre-shared** registration, not
on a hardware root of trust certifying that key at attestation time. The Option-A model
(attestation-carried, issuer-certified keys) slots in behind the **same** `KeyResolver` seam
without changing SINDRI (§6.4.1). This residual is tracked as the platform-attestation half of
**RISK-2026-0005** and the "declared roots of trust are pre-shared" residual in ARCH §13. It is
named, not eliminated. `CONF-2026-07-16-P3-R1`'s M-8 verdict is updated from partial toward
satisfied **for the gate-verification half**, with the pre-shared-roots residual explicit.

## 3. OQGF-M-9 — the guarantee holds at two layers (the honest test story, §7)

The prompt's `test_oqgf_m_9_broadened_chain_is_anergy` targets a state that is **unrepresentable
through the committed input type**: `Sindri::evaluate` takes an `&IntentProvenanceChain`, whose
only construction paths are `new` + `extend`, and `extend` refuses a non-subset `emitted_scope`
(I-3 / OQGF-M-9). There is no `serde`/deserialization path on `IntentProvenanceChain`. So a
broadened chain **cannot be built** to hand to SINDRI — a stronger guarantee than a runtime
test would give. The M-9 guarantee is enforced at two layers, and this check does not overclaim:

1. **Construction (I-3):** a broadened `IntentProvenanceChain` is unrepresentable. Proven by
   `brokkr-core`'s own tests and Phase-3 `test_i3_attenuate_rejects_broadening`.
2. **Verification of a reconstructed chain (`verify_chain_public`):** re-checks subset and maps
   `WouldBroaden`. Proven at the verifier level, where a raw entries slice **can** be tampered:
   `brokkr-intent/tests/chain_public.rs::test_verify_chain_public_rejects_tampered_entry`.

SINDRI's `map_intent_error` carries the `WouldBroaden → ChainInvalid` arm (source-visible) for
the day a deserialization/IPC path is added (a later phase). At the SINDRI boundary today, the
reachable `ChainInvalid` arms are `EntrySignatureInvalid` and `BrokenLink`, both tested
(`test_tampered_entry_signature_is_anergy`, `test_broken_hash_link_is_anergy`). **What is not
tested at this boundary — a broadened chain reaching SINDRI — is not tested because it cannot
occur.** This is stated, not glossed.

## 4. Deferred conjuncts 3–4 — structurally unreachable (confirmed)

`AnergyReason::OutOfScope` and `AnergyReason::InvariantViolated` are **never constructed** in
`brokkr-gate/src` (verified: `grep` finds them only in doc comments explaining the deferral).
`evaluate` returns `Ok(())` after Signals 1–2, and `map_intent_error` produces only
`IdentityUnverified` / `ChainExpired` / `ChainInvalid`. So the two deferred conjuncts cannot be
reached from SINDRI at Phase 4 — the deferral is enforced by the absence of any constructing
code path, not by a runtime flag. The Deferred-Conjunct Deadline (Rev 1.3 §6.4) governs their
landing before Phase 11 (**RISK-2026-0004**).
