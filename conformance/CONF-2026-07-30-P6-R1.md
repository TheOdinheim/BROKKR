## 2. Structural invariants (for when the build proceeds)

- **I-2 (no `Deny → Allow`) — will hold.** `BarrierVerdict` (committed) has no conversion; its
  `compile_fail,E0599` doctest stands. The barrier would return the committed variants and add no
  downgrade path.
- **I-5 (no dependency on `brokkr-genome`) — designed to hold.** The endpoint ceiling arrives through
  a barrier-owned resolver trait, not a crate dependency (§6.5). Planned deps: `brokkr-core` +
  `brokkr-crypto` only. N/A until the crate exists.
- **I-6 (no model calls) — will hold.** Pure logic; no network, no FFI.
