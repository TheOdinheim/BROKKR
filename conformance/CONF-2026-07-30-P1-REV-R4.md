## 0. Scope

## 3. The contribution, stated as representability (§7)

The defect Rev 1.7 fixed was in condition 1: Rev 1.6's egress short-circuit `classification == Public`
would `Allow` a **Public personal** datum with no custody, defeating OQGF-P-11.1 (which binds at every
tier). Fixing it requires `evaluate` to see whether a datum is personal even when no BCR is presented
— so the tag must live on the **flow**, not only on the record. This revision places that field
(`Egress::personal`, `Ingress::personal`) and the BCR field the condition-9 match needs
(`BoundaryCustodyRecord::personal`). The corrected conditions are **Phase 6**; this revision makes
them expressible.
