## 2. Also verified (Step-0 readings and structure)

- **Domain separation** (Task 1) — the BCR tag (`brokkr-barrier:bcr:v1`), acceptance tag
  (`brokkr-barrier:risk-acceptance:v1`), and every `brokkr-genome:*` tag differ; a signature over one
  does not verify as another (`test_bcr_and_acceptance_domain_separated`).
- **AcceptedRisk entry id** — supplied by the acceptance resolver as `(RiskAcceptanceId, RiskAcceptance)`
  (the Organ-5 register key), never synthesized by the Barrier.
- **Multiple failing conditions** — the finding names the **first-failing condition in §6.5 order**.
- **Acceptance on Quarantine** — does **not** apply: a Quarantine carries no finding/`finding_id` and
  is not a Deny (the remedy is provenance, OQGF-I-11). Only the egress `Deny` consults the acceptance
  resolver.
