## 2. Enhanced-level obligations (AMD-001 §AMD.2)

- "Monotonic Intent Attenuation cryptographically enforced (OQGF-M-9)" — **met** (signature over canonical scope + hash-linking; §1 M-9).
- "Intent Invariant Enforcement at every hop (OQGF-M-10)" — **add-not-remove met**; action-evaluation is Phase 4.
- "documented least-privilege Root Intent scoping (OQGF-M-13)" — scope present/signed; documentation-of-broad-scope is Phase 5.
- Chain signatures: BROKKR uses **dual-family (ML-DSA-65 + SLH-DSA-SHAKE-192s)** on every root and entry — this meets the **High-Assurance** dual-family bar (AMD-002/OQGF-M-2), exceeding the Enhanced single-family minimum. Verified by the real `DualKeyPair`.

## 6. Honesty notes (§7)

- The chain carries no public-key material, so `verify_chain` takes the hop verifying keys
  as parameters; their provenance (HW-RoT-rooted attestation, OQGF-M-1) is Phase 4. This is
  stated plainly, not hidden — Phase 3 proves the chain-integrity cryptography *given* the
  keys.
- No completeness claim. Real dual-family signing/hashing (no mocks) proves the M-9 bar;
  later phases prove their own obligations at their own gates.
