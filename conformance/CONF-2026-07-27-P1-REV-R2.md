## 2. I-5 and I-10 — structural checks

**I-5 (the spine depends on nothing internal) — HELD.** The field is `Vec<Capability>`;
`Capability` is already imported from `crate::intent` (used by `ToolEntry::required_capabilities`
and `InvariantEntry::forbids_capabilities` since Rev 1.4). No import added, no dependency added —
`brokkr-core/Cargo.toml` `[dependencies]` remains empty. `Capability` is a same-crate type; no cycle.

**I-10 (required-field shape) — HELD.** `capabilities` is a required `Vec<Capability>`, **not an
`Option`**. A `PolicyRegister` without a vocabulary is unrepresentable, consistent with the
required-field discipline the genome already enforces. `Genome::new`'s arity is unchanged (10) —
`PolicyRegister` is constructed separately — so the three genome `compile_fail,E0308` doctests are
unaffected and still pass (verified by running: 12 doctests, unchanged).
