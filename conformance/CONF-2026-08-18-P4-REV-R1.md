## 1. AMD-001 — OQGF-M-14 (Intent Chain Freshness)

> *"Intent Provenance Chains SHALL carry a freshness nonce and an expiry. A chain whose expiry has
> passed SHALL NOT authorize action and SHALL require re-issuance …"*

| Aspect | Implementation | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| SINDRI checks chain expiry against the **call-site** time, not a stored clock | `Sindri` holds only `resolver`; `evaluate(&self, …, now)` threads `now` → `signal_2(chain, now)` → `Skuld::verify_chain_public(…, now)`. No `self.now`, no `now` field. | `test_oqgf_m_14_i13_expiry_is_evaluated_against_call_time_not_a_held_clock` |

**Verdict: SATISFIED at SINDRI — and the proof is the two-verdict test, not the signature.** One
`Sindri` instance evaluates one chain (expiry 10 000) **twice**: at `now = 500` it **grants**; at
`now = 20 000` it yields **`ChainExpired`**. A clock fixed at construction can produce only one
verdict per chain from a single instance, so this test **cannot pass against the pre-I-13 design** —
it fails exactly where the old test could not. Adding `now` to the signature is necessary but is
*not* the proof; the proof is that the same instance now produces two different freshness verdicts.

**Relationship to the two prior M-14 verdicts (§5.3, referenced not restated).** Phase 4's
`CONF-2026-07-24-P4-R2` recorded M-14 satisfied on a test that injected `now` at construction and
evaluated once — a test that shared the defect's assumption. The `brokkr-core` I-13 revision
(`CONF-2026-08-18-P1-REV-R10`) already corrected that verdict **downward to PARTIAL**, noting core
could require the call-site time but not prove an implementor forwards it. **This revision supplies
exactly that missing proof for SINDRI:** the forwarding is now demonstrated, so M-14 is genuinely
satisfied *at the costimulation gate*. The correction is not restated here; this record is the
forward move it anticipated.

**Scope note — this does not close RISK-2026-0006.** M-14 (intent-chain freshness) is satisfied at
SINDRI. The held-clock **class** also reached BIFRÖST (whose held clock governs BCR expiry under
OQGF-I-9, a different requirement), so the workspace held-clock grep is not yet empty and the risk
stays Open until the Phase 8.5 rebuild lands. See Task 6 / RISK-2026-0006.

## 2. AMD-001 — OQGF-M-11 (Costimulation Gate)

> *"No actor SHALL be granted privileged action on the basis of identity attestation (Signal 1)
> alone. A privileged action SHALL require both a valid identity attestation … and a valid Intent
> Provenance Chain (Signal 2) …"*

**Verdict: PARTIAL — unchanged by this revision.** This revision alters **how the current time
arrives**, and nothing about **what is checked**. Signal 1 (resolve-to-declared-root + bind-to-
proven-hop) and Signal 2 (`verify_chain_public` over declared public roots) are byte-for-byte the
same logic; the Signal-2 call now receives `now` as a parameter instead of reading `self.now`. The
conjunct-3 (action-in-scope) and conjunct-4 (action-respects-invariants) deferral under the
Deferred-Conjunct Deadline (ARCH Rev 1.3 §6.4) is **untouched** — `_action` is still unused, and
`OutOfScope`/`InvariantViolated` remain unreachable from `evaluate` by construction. Proof that the
signals are intact: the full negative suite still passes unchanged — `undeclared_identity`,
`identity_does_not_bind` (load-bearing), `wrong_principal`, `unresolvable_hop`,
`tampered_entry_signature`, `broken_hash_link`, plus the two positive grants.
