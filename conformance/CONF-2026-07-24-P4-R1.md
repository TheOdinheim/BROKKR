## 0. What was checked, and against what (§7)

This is not a zero-findings report. I enumerated the AMD-001 §AMD.1 requirements in scope for
the Costimulation Gate and, for each, checked whether it can be **built from the committed
types** as they stand. Two requirements' clauses are **absent** from the committed surface
(action-scope-fit and action-invariant-evaluation), which under §5.3 is a §4 gap and stops the
build. The gaps are `GAP-2026-07-24-001` (action semantics — halting) and `GAP-2026-07-24-002`
(attestation intrinsic-signature encoding — secondary). Applicable level: **Enhanced**
(AMD-001 §AMD.2).

## 2. OQGF-M-8 disposition and RISK-2026-0003

This phase is where M-8 would move from **partial** toward satisfied — but only the part SINDRI
actually closes. **What the resolver provides (Option B):** a *declared registry* of
`(SubjectId → DualPublicKey)` roots of trust, supplied directly to `RegistryResolver`. Chain
verification against those declared roots is the closable half. **What remains as residual
(Option A):** keys carried in, and certified by, the attestation itself (attestation-carried
issuer-certified keys), so trust is rooted in an issuer chain rather than a static declared
list. Because **no gate was built this round**, M-8 stays **partial** in
`CONF-2026-07-16-P3-R1`, and RISK-2026-0003's residual stays **OPEN**, target Phase 4 — now
further specified: the SKULD verify path and the `DualPublicKey` type both exist; the remaining
step is SINDRI invoking them through the resolver, which is blocked only by GAP-…-001 (the
gate's action-semantics), not by anything on the key-resolution seam.

## 5. Findings

- **absent: 2 clauses** — the M-11/M-10 **action-in-scope** and **action-respects-invariants**
  conjuncts are absent from the committed type surface (no `Action → Capability` map; no
  `(Action, Invariant) → bool` predicate). Filed as **GAP-2026-07-24-001**. Per §5.3 the build
  **stops**.
- **partial (secondary):** M-1 intrinsic attestation-signature verification has no committed
  canonical content. Filed as **GAP-2026-07-24-002** with a non-inventing construction
  recommended (bind Signal 1 to Signal 2).
- **buildable, not built: 3** — M-8 (chain side), M-9, M-14 are all enforced *inside*
  `verify_chain_public`; SINDRI would enforce them by calling it. They await the gate, which
  awaits GAP-…-001.
- **No requirement relaxed. No convention invented. No completeness claim.** The gate was not
  written, because writing `evaluate` requires deciding steps 3–4, and that decision is the
  DAP's (GAP-…-001 §3).
