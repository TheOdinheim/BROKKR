## Method, and its honest limits (read this before the tables)

This check runs in the direction CLAUDE.md Section 5.3 requires: **the requirement text
is the ground truth, the architecture is the subject.** Requirements were enumerated by
walking the normative sections of the corpus. The architecture's own traceability table
(BROKKR-ARCH Section 13) was **not** used as the enumeration source — that table is part
of the subject being audited, and checking it against itself is the circular audit
Section 7 forbids. Where a table row below cites Section 13, it cites it as *a claim the
architecture makes* (which I then test), never as evidence.

**Enumeration unit.** The unit is the **numbered normative requirement** (`OQGF-G-1`,
`OQGF-M-11`, `OQGF-P-8.5`, `OQGF-I-10`, and so on) — 85 in total. Many of these bundle
several `SHALL` clauses; where a requirement bundles clauses, I checked the operative
`SHALL`s inside it and the verdict reflects the weakest one. I did **not** extract every
individual `SHALL` token in the corpus as its own row — a fully atomized pass would run to
several hundred rows and is not what makes this useful. This is stated so the DAP can see
exactly what depth was and was not reached: the numbered requirements are covered; the
sub-clause `SHALL`s inside each are checked but not each given its own row.

**Verdict vocabulary.**
- **SPECIFIED** — the architecture says enough about a BROKKR-specific hook (subsystem,
  crate, type) to build from.
- **PARTIAL** — the architecture gestures at it but does not say enough to build, or
  satisfies part and is silent on the rest. Called out plainly; PARTIAL is more useful to
  the DAP than a generous SPECIFIED.
- **ABSENT** — the architecture neither specifies a hook nor declares the requirement
  inapplicable. Per Section 5.3, each ABSENT is a gap and the build stops. The eight
  ABSENT findings are consolidated in `gaps/GAP-2026-07-14-001.md`.
- **N.A.** — inapplicable. Marked **(declared)** where BROKKR-ARCH Section 1.4 declares it,
  or **(justified here)** where it is inapplicable by its own terms (e.g., a requirement
  about assessors, or a High-Assurance-only obligation on an Enhanced system). A
  requirement that is *plausibly* inapplicable but **not declared** is NOT counted N.A. —
  it is ABSENT, because "silently missing" and "declared inapplicable" are different things
  (Section 5.3), and choosing the inapplicable reading myself would be picking the reading
  that unblocks me (Section 4).

**Conformance level.** BROKKR is **Enhanced (OQGF-E)** (CLAUDE.md Section 1; BROKKR-ARCH
Section 1.4). Enhanced is the binding column wherever a requirement's obligation varies by
level.

**Totals: 52 SPECIFIED · 16 PARTIAL · 8 ABSENT · 9 N.A.** (85 requirements). The
architecture is strong on the amendment layer it was revised to close (AMD-001 through
AMD-007 are almost entirely SPECIFIED). The findings cluster in **OQGF-1.0's Organ-level
operational requirements**, several of which the architecture neither maps to a BROKKR
hook nor declares n.a.
