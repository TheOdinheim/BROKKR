## 1. AMD-005 — resolution (OQGF-P-8.5, OQGF-P-8.2)

> **P-8.5:** *"Autonomous action MAY raise posture but SHALL NOT autonomously de-escalate above Baseline…
> DAP confirmation. Where uncertain, it SHALL remain escalated."* — enforced by the signature over the
> decision, plus (Rev 1.12) freshness so a captured decision cannot be replayed.

| Aspect | Placed here? | Where enforcement lands |
|---|---|---|
| **What the signature covers** — a defined encoding of `escalation, cleared_condition, dap, at, nonce, expiry` | **Yes** — `resolution_signed_content`, in core, under `brokkr-core:resolution:v1` | this revision |
| **Freshness** — `nonce` + `expiry` on the decision | **Yes** — two required fields | this revision |
| **Verify** the signature under the declared DAP key; refuse a decision that does not verify | **No** | `brokkr-sentinel` (EIR `resolve`) |
| **Refuse** `now > expiry`; **refuse** a replayed `nonce` for that escalation | **No** | `brokkr-sentinel` (EIR) |

**Verdict: type surface placed; P-8.5 NOT claimed satisfied.** *"A requirement enforced by a signature over
undefined bytes is not enforced"* (§6.8) — this revision defines the bytes and the freshness fields, so the
enforcement the sentinel already performs now has a shared, canonical encoding and a nonce/expiry to check.
Proof the surface is present and correct: the three encoding tests (deterministic, unambiguous,
signature-excluded) pass.

> **P-8.2:** *"Return to the Baseline Posture SHALL be an explicit, recorded decision… not an implicit or
> silent timeout."*

**Verdict: traced — unchanged.** The `nonce`/`expiry` are **freshness** (an upper bound on a decision's
validity), not a **timeout to baseline**: nothing resolves *because* a timer expired; an expired decision
simply stops being able to resolve. Resolution remains an explicit signed act. This revision does not make
resolution a timeout.

## 2. AMD-002 — peripheral tolerance (OQGF-P-4)

> *"…PQC-signed… Expired or out-of-scope grants SHALL have no effect."*

**Verdict: type surface placed; not satisfied here.** `ToleranceError::SignatureInvalid` is placed with its
corpus-grounded message, so the sentinel can report a forged grant **as forged** rather than as
`OutOfScope` — one true, first-failing reason. The *return* of this variant on a real signature failure is
the sentinel's `grant_heuristic` (the follow-on revision); this revision places the variant only. The doc
records the ordered-checks / first-failing-reason rule (§6.7) so the sentinel implements it against a
committed contract.

## 3. AMD-001 — freshness pattern (OQGF-M-14) as it bears

> *"Intent Provenance Chains SHALL carry a freshness nonce and an expiry."*

**Verdict: traced.** M-14 established the nonce+expiry freshness pattern for the intent chain. Rev 1.12
applies the **same** pattern to the resolution decision — the last signed artifact that lacked it — so a
decision, like a chain, a grant, a signal, a risk acceptance, and a BCR, now carries a nonce and an expiry.
This is a consistency gain across the signed-artifact surface, not a new M-14 obligation.
