## Structural invariant I-13 — a gate that evaluates an expiry takes `now` as a parameter of the evaluating call

`screen` now takes `now: Timestamp` per call and stamps `produced_at: now`; it does not read a wall
clock and does not store the time. The four-pattern held-clock grep (`SystemTime::now`, `Instant::now`,
`self.now`, `Mutex<Timestamp>`, `set_now`, `now_guard`) is **empty** across all `brokkr-*/src`; every
`now: Timestamp` in `brokkr-sentinel/src` is a call parameter (`observe`, `screen`, EIR's inherent
methods), never a struct field. **brokkr-sentinel holds no clock.**

**Verdict: satisfied (this crate holds no clock; the newly added time is a per-call parameter).**
