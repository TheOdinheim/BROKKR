## 2. I-5 and I-10 — the structural checks

**I-5 (the spine depends on nothing it governs) — HELD, and it is the raw-bytes choice that preserves it.**
`RootOfTrustEntry` declares `ml_dsa_public: Vec<u8>` / `slh_dsa_public: Vec<u8>` — **raw bytes**, not
`DualPublicKey`. `DualPublicKey` lives in `brokkr-crypto`, which depends on `brokkr-core`; a core
register holding it would invert the dependency and break I-5. Verified: `brokkr-core/Cargo.toml`
`[dependencies]` is **empty** — no internal (or external) dependency was added. The revision imports
only from *within* `brokkr-core` (`crate::intent::{Capability, Invariant}`, `crate::crypto::{SignatureAlg,
HashAlg, KemAlg}`, `crate::ids::SubjectId`).

**I-10 (an incomplete genome is unrepresentable) — HELD, and extended to the two new registers.**
`roots` and `policy` are required fields (no `Option`), and `Genome::new` requires all ten arguments.
Proven by a **new compile-fail doctest**: `Genome::new(…, None, …)` with `None` at the `roots`
position fails to compile with **E0308** (expected `RootsOfTrust`, found `Option<_>`). The original
signature doctest was updated from 8 to 10 arguments so it still fails on the **signature type**
(E0308), not on arity (E0061) — a doctest that failed for arity would prove nothing. Both error
codes were verified by running the doctests (rustdoc checks the emitted code), not guessed.
