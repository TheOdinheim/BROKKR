## 1. AMD-001 — OQGF-M-14 / AMD-007 — OQGF-I-9 (the expiry clause)

> **M-14:** *"A chain whose expiry has passed SHALL NOT authorize action …"* — **I-9:** *"A BCR that
> is unsigned, malformed, or **expired** SHALL NOT authorize a crossing."*

BIFRÖST delegates the egress decision to HÚÐ, whose expiry check (egress condition 5, `now <=
bcr.expiry`) now receives the **call-site** `now` that `ContextClearance::evaluate_context(&self,
ctx, dest, now)` threads through — not a stored clock.

| Aspect | Implementation | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| BCR expiry evaluated against the call time, not a held clock | `Bifrost` holds only `{ huth, ceiling }`; `evaluate_context(&self, ctx, dest, now)` passes `now` to `self.huth.evaluate(&flow, now)`. No held field, no interior mutability. | `test_oqgf_m_14_i13_i9_bcr_expiry_evaluated_against_call_time_not_a_held_clock` |

**Verdict: SATISFIED — on the two-verdict test, not the signature.** One `Bifrost` instance, one
context and destination, `clear` called twice: at `now = 1_000` (< BCR expiry 5_000) it **clears**
(mints a `ClearedContext`); at `now = 10_000` (> 5_000) it **Denies** with the `Expired` condition. A
held clock is one stored value and cannot produce two verdicts from one instance, so this test cannot
pass against the pre-I-13 design. This is the forward move the prior R1 record could not make — R1's
crate held its clock and its tests injected `now` at construction and evaluated once, sharing the
defect's assumption.

## 5. The workspace held-clock grep — RISK-2026-0006's closure tool — and what it found

Task 4 ran the four-pattern grep across **all** crates (the risk's closure condition is an empty
*workspace* grep). Result:

- `self\.now`, `Mutex<Timestamp>`, `fn now(` — **empty workspace-wide.**
- `now:.*Timestamp,` — matches only **function parameters** (the per-call `now` I-13 mandates) in
  core/gate/intent/barrier/genome/bifrost — **except two struct fields**: `EirState.now`
  (`brokkr-sentinel/src/eir.rs:54`) and `HeimdallState.now` (`heimdall.rs:42`).

**These two are held clocks, and both engines check an expiry against them** — EIR the
resolution-decision expiry (`eir.rs:242`, OQGF-P-8.5, the act that lowers a defence) and HEIMDALL the
tolerance-grant expiry (`heimdall.rs:268`, OQGF-P-4). This is the I-13 defect in a **third** crate,
which predates I-13 (sentinel is Phase 8; I-13 is Rev 1.15). It is **out of this revision's scope**
(the scope lock forbids modifying `brokkr-sentinel`) and is filed as **GAP-2026-08-18-001** with a
recommendation for a DAP-placed sentinel I-13 revision.

**Consequence for I-13 and RISK-2026-0006 (recorded, §5.3 — a verdict may move backward):** I-13
**holds for `brokkr-bifrost`** (this crate) but **does not hold workspace-wide** — `brokkr-sentinel`
violates it. Any prior conformance record that recorded OQGF-P-4 or OQGF-P-8.5 satisfied did so with
fixed-`now` fixtures that could not have caught the held clock; those verdicts are now provisional
pending the sentinel revision. RISK-2026-0006 is therefore **not closed** — its closure condition
(empty workspace grep) is unmet.
