## The I-2 doctest — verified to prove the RIGHT thing (the Task-5 trap)

Reshaping `BarrierFinding` (adding `datum`, `condition`) made its inline construction in the I-2
`compile_fail,E0599` doctest miss two fields. Left unfixed, the doctest would have failed on **E0063
(missing field)** while `compile_fail` stayed green — proving nothing about I-2. The construction was
updated to the four-field shape, and the emitted error code was **read from the compiler**, not
guessed: compiling the doctest body standalone emits **`error[E0599]` and nothing else** — the
construction compiles and only `.into_allow()` fails. The doctest proves I-2 (no `Deny → Allow`), and
`cargo test --doc` passing confirms rustdoc sees E0599.
