  `brokkr-intent/tests/chain_public.rs::test_verify_chain_public_rejects_tampered_entry`. The
  reachable `ChainInvalid` arms (`EntrySignatureInvalid`, `BrokenLink`) are tested here.
- **Conjuncts 3 (action-in-scope) and 4 (action-respects-invariants) are not tested — they are
  deferred** under the Rev 1.3 Deferred-Conjunct Deadline (Phase 5 / before Phase 11). SINDRI
