## Workspace build — intended breakage confirmed

`cargo build --locked --workspace` fails, by design. brokkr-crypto, brokkr-intent, brokkr-barrier,
brokkr-audit, brokkr-genome, brokkr-sentinel all compile. Only the two implementors of the changed
traits break:

```
error[E0050]: method `evaluate` has 4 parameters but the declaration in trait
              `brokkr_core::CostimulationGate::evaluate` has 5   --> brokkr-gate/src/sindri.rs:150:9
error[E0050]: method `evaluate_context` has 3 parameters but the declaration in trait
              `evaluate_context` has 4                            --> brokkr-bifrost/src/lib.rs:200:25
```

**This is a known, intended consequence.** The two follow-on revisions are scoped by these exact
errors: `brokkr-gate` threads `now` into SINDRI's freshness check (replacing the held `self.now`)
and updates 10 `authorize` calls in `tests/costimulation.rs`; the Phase 8.5 rebuild adds `now` to
`brokkr-bifrost`'s `evaluate_context` (passing it to `Barrier::evaluate`) and to `clear` calls in
`tests/bifrost.rs`. The test-level call-site errors surface only after the lib E0050s are fixed.
