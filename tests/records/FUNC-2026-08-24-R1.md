## Step-0 correction worth recording

The 14C report cited **35664** bytes for the SLH-DSA-SHAKE-192s signature; that is the **192f** (fast) variant. The linked `libwolfssl` reports **16224** for **192s** (small, FIPS-205), verified by probe before adding the F-18 guard — using 35664 would have rejected every legitimate key.
