# GAP-2026-08-18-002 — The brokkr-sentinel I-13 fix requires a brokkr-core trait revision first

**Filed:** 18 August 2026
**Phase:** brokkr-sentinel I-13 revision (EIR + HEIMDALL stop holding the clock)
**Filed by:** the builder, at Task 1 — after grounding and a reverted compiler experiment
**Status:** OPEN — build STOPPED; no source written to `brokkr-sentinel` (the experiment was reverted;
the crate is clean)
**Why stopped:** the fix cannot be done within `brokkr-sentinel` alone. Every held-clock read is
inside a **brokkr-core trait method**, and giving those methods a per-call `now` changes the
**core** traits — which the scope lock forbids ("Touch brokkr-sentinel ONLY. Do NOT modify any other
crate"), and which the prompt's own instruction covers: *"if the break is in another crate, STOP and
report rather than reaching outside scope."*

---

## What the fix requires, and why it leaves scope

The revision must make EIR's and HEIMDALL's time-dependent decisions read the current time as a
**per-call parameter** (I-13), not from held state. The four held-clock read sites are:

| Crate site | Method | Reads | Comparison it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `eir.rs:232` | `ResolutionEngine::resolve` | `let now = st.now` | decision expiry `now.0 > d.expiry.0` (eir.rs:242, OQGF-P-8.5) |
| `eir.rs:183` | `ResolutionEngine::may_resolve` | `let now = st.now` → `classify(now, e)` | dwell/hold/max (eir.rs:135–156, OQGF-P-8.3) |
| `eir.rs:284` | `ResolutionEngine::scan_chronic` | `let now = st.now` | chronic max_duration (eir.rs:289, OQGF-P-8.6) |
| `heimdall.rs:268` | `ToleranceController::grant_heuristic` | `self.state().now` | grant expiry `now > grant.expiry` (OQGF-P-4) |

**All four are trait methods whose traits live in `brokkr-core`:**

- `ResolutionEngine` (`brokkr-core/src/resolution.rs:261`): `may_resolve(&self, escalation)`,
  `resolve(&self, decision)`, `scan_chronic(&self)` — **none takes `now`.**
- `ToleranceController` (`brokkr-core/src/tolerance.rs:161`): `grant_heuristic(&self, grant)` — **does
  not take `now`.**

To make `resolve` read a per-call `now`, `ResolutionEngine::resolve` must take `now` — a change to a
core trait. Same for `may_resolve`, `scan_chronic`, and `grant_heuristic`.

## Compiler evidence (a grep is not a proof; the compiler naming it is)

Making `EIR::resolve` take `now: Timestamp` per call, as Task 1 directs, and building (then reverted):

```
error[E0050]: method `resolve` has 3 parameters but the declaration in trait `resolve` has 2
   --> brokkr-sentinel/src/eir.rs:224:16
error: could not compile `brokkr-sentinel` (lib) due to 1 previous error
```

The trait `ResolutionEngine::resolve` declares two parameters; the impl cannot add a third without the
trait changing. The identical E0050 would arise for `may_resolve`, `scan_chronic`, and
`grant_heuristic`. **There is no in-scope partial fix:** every held-clock read is in a trait method, so
none can be corrected without the core trait carrying `now`.

## Why the prior corrections did not hit this, and why this one does

SINDRI (Phase 4 revision) and BIFRÖST (Phase 8.5 rebuild) could be fixed **within their own crates**
only because the core traits they implement — `CostimulationGate` and `ContextClearance` — had
**already** been revised to add `now` by the `brokkr-core` I-13 revision (commit `7d47d2a`,
`PHASE-1-REV-2026-08-18-R10`). That core revision added `now` to `gate.rs` and `reasoner.rs`
**only**; it did **not** touch `resolution.rs` or `tolerance.rs`. So the core prerequisite for the
sentinel fix was never placed. This gap is that missing prerequisite, surfaced.

## Recommendation (for DAP decision) — the three-commit pattern, again

1. **Place a `brokkr-core` trait revision** adding `now: Timestamp` (last parameter, matching the
   `CostimulationGate`/`ContextClearance`/`Barrier` convention) to:
   - `ResolutionEngine::may_resolve`, `::resolve`, `::scan_chronic` (`resolution.rs`);
   - `ToleranceController::grant_heuristic` (`tolerance.rs`).
   This is the same shape as `7d47d2a`, extended to the two Physiology traits Phase 8 owns. It will
   break `brokkr-sentinel` with E0050s (as `7d47d2a` broke gate and bifrost) — that breakage is the
   scoping signal for step 2, not a defect.
2. **Re-issue the `brokkr-sentinel` I-13 revision** (this task) against the revised traits: delete the
   `now` field and `set_now` from both engines; thread the call-site `now` through `may_resolve`,
   `resolve`, `scan_chronic`, and `grant_heuristic`; feed `classify` the call parameter rather than
   `st.now`; add the two-verdict tests (EIR decision-expiry across its expiry with the nonce handled;
   HEIMDALL grant-expiry — the fails-toward-blindness case; EIR hysteresis ages).
3. **The `Eir::new`/`Heimdall::new` `now` constructor parameters** also go away in step 2 (they seed
   the field being deleted); that is a public-API change internal to `brokkr-sentinel` and needs no
   core coordination beyond step 1.

## What this gap does NOT do

- It does **not** modify `brokkr-sentinel` (the experiment above is reverted; the crate is clean) or
  reach into `brokkr-core` to place the trait change myself — that is a DAP-placed revision under the
  three-commit discipline (§5.2), and picking the reading that lets me proceed by editing core would
  be the §4-forbidden move.
- It does **not** close RISK-2026-0006 — the sentinel half remains, now with its blocker identified.
