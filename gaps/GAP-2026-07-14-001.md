BROKKR hook nor declared `n.a.` in Section 1.4.** The architecture uses a declared-`n.a.`
mechanism (Section 1.4) but applies it only to the quantum-specific requirements (I-3, M-3,
A-2, A-4, R-5, R-7). The eight below fall through that gap. Per CLAUDE.md Section 5.3, a
requirement that is *silently missing* is not the same as one *declared inapplicable*, and I
may not resolve the difference by choosing the reading that lets me proceed (Section 4).

## 3. Why I cannot proceed without inventing something

To carry these into Phase 1 I would have to decide, for each, either that it does not apply
to a coding agent (declare `n.a.` on the DAP's behalf) or how BROKKR satisfies it (invent a
hook the architecture does not contain). Both are the builder placing a decision that
CLAUDE.md Section 1 reserves to the architecture and the DAP: *"You do not decide what BROKKR
should be,"* and *"the builder must never be the channel by which its own governing
specification changes"* (Section 0). Several of these are *plausibly* `n.a.` for a coding
agent — but "plausibly n.a. and undeclared" is exactly the silently-missing case Section 5.3
names, and adopting the plausible reading myself is the Section-4-forbidden move of choosing
what unblocks me.

## 4. Recommendation (a recommendation only — I do not adopt it)

I recommend the DAP dispose of each finding by one of three routes, and place the result as
a revision of the architecture (never me):

- **Declare `n.a.` in Section 1.4 with justification** — most likely correct for I-1, I-2,
  I-5 (HNDL TLS-monitoring is a network-appliance function, arguably not a coding agent's),
  M-6 (fold into the R-2 model-provider reinterpretation), and A.6.1 (an operations
  document, not a property of the build artifact).
- **Add a BROKKR hook** — most likely correct for G-7 (a key-lifecycle note in the crypto
  section / Phase 2), M-5 (state the mutual-auth posture for the reasoner-API channel), and
  R-6 (state signing-key custody, or declare `n.a.` at Enhanced as HA-tier).
- **Defer with a dated marker** — for any the DAP judges real but out of scope for the
  current phases, recorded as a deferral rather than left silent.

This gap **adds** clarity and **relaxes nothing**. No requirement is weakened by resolving
it; each disposition either binds a hook or records an explicit inapplicability.
