## 1. What I was doing

Beginning Phase 9: build `brokkr-adapt` (KVASIR) at the surface placed by Architecture Rev 1.17 §6.12 — the corpus seam (Task 1), the four poisoning gates (Tasks 2–5), and canonical serialization of `DetectorProvenance` (Task 6). ## 2. The blocker — `AdaptError` has no variant for a corpus-validity failure

`MaturationPipeline::select` returns `Result<SelectionPass, AdaptError>`, so **every refusal is an `Err(AdaptError)`**. The committed `AdaptError` (`brokkr-core/src/adapt.rs`, unchanged) has exactly five variants:

| Variant | Meaning (from its message) |
|---|---|
| `UnconfirmedSeed` | *"refinement may only be seeded by a DAP-confirmed incident (OQGF-P-6.1)"* — Gate 1 |
| `Overfit` | *"candidate improves only on its seeding sample (OQGF-P-6.2)"* — a **candidate-quality** verdict |
| `CoverageRegression` | *"candidate degrades coverage elsewhere in the corpus (OQGF-P-6.2)"* — a **candidate-quality** verdict |
| `FailsTolerance` | *"candidate raises host harm above the bound (OQGF-P-6.3)"* — Gate 3 |
| `NeedsApproval` | *"activation requires DAP approval at Enhanced (OQGF-P-6.6)"* — Gate 4 |

§6.12 requires Gate 2 to reject a **substituted corpus** *before measuring anything*:

> *"**Selection SHALL verify the corpus digest against the declared record before measuring anything**, for the reason screening verifies one: otherwise a candidate could be selected against a substituted corpus chosen to make it look good. That check is the binding between the declared corpus and the evidence actually used."*

The prompt makes this a required negative test:

> *"test_oqgf_p_6_2_substituted_corpus_refused — a corpus whose digest does not match the declared record is refused and NOTHING is measured."*

**None of the five variants honestly expresses "this corpus is not the valid, current corpus."** A substituted corpus is a *corpus-validity* failure — the evidence is untrustworthy — categorically distinct from a *candidate-quality* verdict. Reusing `Overfit` or `CoverageRegression` for it is not merely imprecise; it is **a positive false claim about the candidate**:

- A substituted corpus is *"chosen to make the candidate look good."* When it is caught by the digest check, **nothing about the candidate has been measured** — the candidate may be genuinely excellent or genuinely poor; we cannot tell, because the evidence is invalid.
- `Overfit` asserts *"the candidate improves only on its seeding sample."* `CoverageRegression` asserts *"the candidate degrades coverage elsewhere."* Both are specific negative findings about the candidate that **were not established**. Refusing an excellent candidate because its corpus was poisoned, and recording `Overfit` against it, **libels a good candidate and misreports a poisoning attack as a candidate defect.**

This is the **exact** imprecision the DAP has corrected twice:

- **`ToleranceError::SignatureInvalid`** (Rev 1.12) — a forged grant had been reported as `OutOfScope`: *"fail-closed, but untrue in the record, in exactly the place someone investigating an attack would be reading."*
- **`ResolveError::{Expired, ReplayedNonce}`** (Rev 1.13, GAP-2026-08-12-001) — reusing `NeedsDapConfirmation` for a replay *"points the investigation at the one thing that is not wrong."*

A substituted corpus is a **corpus-poisoning attack** (AMD-003's named residual is exactly poisoning). Reporting it as `Overfit` points an investigator at the candidate's generalization when the real event is a compromised corpus source — different events, different responses (re-author the candidate vs. investigate the corpus supply), the same reason `SignatureInvalid ≠ OutOfScope` and `ReplayedNonce ≠ Expired`.

### 2.1 The same gap recurs in `activate` (Task 5), secondary

Task 5 requires, *"by the same rule"* as the stale-screen check:

> *"the selection pass names the current corpus version"* — i.e. `activate` SHALL refuse a candidate whose provenance names a **superseded** corpus version (version equality, not recency, is the gate).

A **stale corpus** at activation is again a corpus-validity failure. `FailsTolerance` (tolerance is about the *Self Set*, not the corpus) and `Overfit`/`CoverageRegression` (candidate quality) all misreport it. There is **no honest variant**. This instance is untested by the prompt but is a required check, so it belongs to the same gap.

**Note the contrast — what is NOT gapped:** the *screen*-version staleness check (`provenance.screen_result.version != current Self Set version`) maps honestly to **`FailsTolerance`** — a pass against a superseded Self Set does not establish tolerance against the *current* one, which is precisely what §6.12 says: *"a candidate ... fails the Self Set screen ... FailsTolerance."* And a **forged provenance signature** at activation maps honestly to **`NeedsApproval`** — because the provenance signature *is* the DAP approval (the `approver` signs it), a signature that does not verify means there is genuinely no valid DAP approval; `NeedsApproval` is true, not a false claim. Those two are buildable and are not part of this gap. The gap is narrowly the **corpus-validity** failures.

## 3. Why I cannot resolve it within scope

Adding a variant to `AdaptError` is a change to **`brokkr-core`**, and Phase 9's scope is *"Build brokkr-adapt and NOTHING else... OUT OF SCOPE: ... Any I/O"* with an explicit *"Do not modify brokkr-core"* posture inherited from every prior phase's scope lock. The only in-scope ways to make `select` refuse a substituted corpus are:

- (a) reuse `Overfit`/`CoverageRegression` — a false claim about the candidate, the imprecision the DAP twice corrected; or
- (b) express corpus-validity via a **crate-local error** at a corpus-verification step, keeping `select`'s `AdaptError` for candidate-quality outcomes only — a plausible design, but it **deviates from the prompt's framing** (the prompt lists `test_oqgf_p_6_2_substituted_corpus_refused` under the P-6.2 tests that *"each assert the specific AdaptError"*), and it decides *how a poisoning attack is surfaced in the record* — a governance-relevant choice, not a mere implementation detail.

Choosing (a) or (b) unilaterally is choosing the reading that unblocks me, which CLAUDE.md §4 forbids. So the phase stops and the choice goes to the DAP.

## 5. Why the whole phase stops (not "build 1, 2, 4, 6 and gap the rest")

Building everything else would produce a KVASIR whose Gate 2 cannot honestly refuse a poisoned corpus — the **first** thing §6.12 says Gate 2 must do (*"verify the corpus digest ... before measuring anything"*), and one of the four poisoning gates the whole subsystem exists to be. The required test `test_oqgf_p_6_2_substituted_corpus_refused` could not be written honestly (it would have to assert a false `Overfit`). A green Phase 9 that misreports corpus poisoning as candidate overfit would be a false completion of exactly the poisoning-resistance property AMD-003 exists to provide — the same reason GAP-2026-08-12-001 declined to ship three buildable tasks with the load-bearing property unenforced. So the phase stops as a whole, no `brokkr-adapt` source is written, and the crate is not added to the workspace, pending the disposition.

## 6. Recommendation (a recommendation only — I do not adopt it)

Placed by the DAP (never me), before Phase 9 builds under it (§5.2 three-commit pattern), as either a **`brokkr-core` revision** (option A) or an **architecture clarification** (option D):

- **(A — additive, preferred) Add corpus-validity variant(s) to `AdaptError`**, mirroring the `SignatureInvalid` and `Expired`/`ReplayedNonce` additions:
  - `SubstitutedCorpus` — *"the evaluation corpus digest does not match the declared record (OQGF-P-6.2)"* (returned by `select` when the recomputed digest ≠ the declared digest); and
  - `StaleCorpus` — *"the evaluation corpus is not the current version (OQGF-P-6.2)"* (returned by `activate` when `provenance.corpus_version` ≠ the current corpus version).

  **Two variants, not one:** a substitution (digest mismatch) is *tampering* — a poisoning attack — while a stale version is *drift* (an old but genuine corpus); a forensic reader wants them distinct, the same judgment that split `Expired` from `ReplayedNonce`. (A single `CorpusInvalid` covering both is the weaker, simpler alternative — it collapses attack and drift.) Whether containment (`corpus contains the seeding sample`) should also route to a corpus-validity variant, or remain `Overfit` (defensible: a corpus containing the seed lets the candidate overfit to its own seed), is a sub-choice for the DAP; I read `Overfit` as honest-enough for containment and would keep it there.

- **(D — clarification, also valid) Rule that corpus validity is a crate-local concern.** Confirm that corpus-integrity/currency is KVASIR input-validation surfaced through a crate-local error (not `AdaptError`), and that the two corpus tests assert that crate-local error. This needs no core change; it is a governance decision about where a poisoning-attack outcome is recorded, which is why it is the DAP's to make and not mine.

**Labeling per §4 (does the recommendation unblock me?): yes — intrinsic, and I say so plainly.** `select` must *refuse* a substituted corpus with an `Err(AdaptError)`, the return type admits only `AdaptError`, and no honest variant exists — so any disposition that lets Phase 9 proceed is one that either adds a variant (A/B) or relocates the error (D). My genuine engineering preference is **(A) with two variants**, because it names the two conditions truthfully, the same reasoning that made `SignatureInvalid` and `ReplayedNonce` distinct; I would prefer it over the simpler combined variant even though the combined one is less work. I adopt none. The choice is the DAP's.

## 7. What is ready the moment this is disposed

The other five tasks are fully designed and determinable and proceed immediately once the corpus-validity refusal has an honest home:

- **Task 1:** `EvaluationCorpusContent` crate-local trait + in-memory test double.
- **Task 2:** `generate` gates the seed against a crate-local `ConfirmedIncidentLedger` seam (reading (c)) and stamps the pipeline-held authored `(base, delta)` (reading (b)) into a `RefinedDetector`; `UnconfirmedSeed` otherwise.
- **Task 3:** `select` verifies the recomputed corpus digest against the declared digest → the disposed corpus-validity refusal; verifies non-containment of the seed sample (reported as *non-containment, not independence*, §13); registers candidate + incumbent runnable detectors, runs both over `samples()`, and refuses `Overfit` (no improvement on the seed's attack class, or improvement only on the seed) / `CoverageRegression` (a benign sample the incumbent cleared that the candidate fires on); returns a `SelectionPass` with the corpus version and `now`.
- **Task 4:** Gate 3 registers the candidate's runnable detector in HEIMDALL and calls `screen(candidate_spec, current_self_set, now)`; `FailsCentralTolerance` → `FailsTolerance`, applied **after** selection succeeds so there is no trade (`test_oqgf_p_6_3_raises_host_harm_refused` is the load-bearing test).
- **Task 5:** `activate` verifies the provenance dual-family signature (`NeedsApproval` on failure), `screen_result.version == current Self Set` (`FailsTolerance` on stale), `corpus_version == current corpus` (the disposed corpus-validity refusal), and returns `PriorGeneration` — reversibility by construction.
- **Task 6:** canonical `provenance_signed_content` under `brokkr-adapt:provenance:v1`, with the five-crate domain-separation test.

Only the corpus-validity refusal this gap asks the DAP to place (or relocate) stands between here and a complete Phase 9. No requirement is relaxed; the ask is to *add* an honest refusal, or to *clarify* where it lives.

## DISPOSITION — 19 August 2026 (annotation per §8; original body above is unchanged)

**Disposed by:** Jeremy Rose, DAP, via the `brokkr-core` Rev R12 revision that places the two variants (`reports/PHASE-1-REV-2026-08-19-R12.md`, `conformance/CONF-2026-08-19-P1-REV-R12.md`). The original body above is preserved verbatim.

**The DAP selected option A — two variants, not one.** `AdaptError` gains **`SubstitutedCorpus`** (*"the evaluation corpus digest does not match the declared record (OQGF-P-6.2)"*, returned by `select` when the recomputed digest ≠ the declared record) and **`StaleCorpus`** (*"the evaluation corpus is not the current version (OQGF-P-6.2)"*, returned by `select` against a superseded corpus and by `activate` when the provenance names a superseded corpus version). This is exactly the two-variant, additive recommendation this gap's §6 preferred.

**Two variants because they are two different events — tampering vs drift.** A substitution is a corpus *chosen to make the candidate look good* — a poisoning attack; a stale version is an *old but genuine* corpus — drift. Collapsing them into one `CorpusInvalid` would file "someone poisoned your evidence" and "your evidence is out of date" under one word. This is the same judgment that split `Expired` from `ReplayedNonce` (Rev 1.13), applied to the corpus.

**Containment stays as `Overfit` — the claim is true.** A corpus that *contains* the seeding sample lets the candidate improve on its own seed, which *is* "improves only on its seeding sample" (`Overfit`'s committed meaning). No new variant is needed there, and none was added; the gap's §6 read `Overfit` as honest for containment and the disposition confirms it. The two new variants are strictly for the *digest-mismatch* (substitution) and *version-mismatch* (drift) cases, where reusing `Overfit`/`CoverageRegression` would be a positive false claim about the candidate.

**The labeled self-interest disclosure was correct, and the labeled option was the one selected — because the unblock is intrinsic.** This gap's §6 flagged that its recommendation **unblocks the builder**, and that the unblock was *intrinsic*: `select` must refuse a substituted corpus with an `Err(AdaptError)`, the return type admits only `AdaptError`, and no honest variant existed — so any disposition that lets Phase 9 proceed adds a variant (A/B) or relocates the error (D). As with GAP-2026-08-12-001, the DAP chose the recommended additive option precisely because there was no non-unblocking correct answer; the labeling correctly said so, and the two-variant preference the report stated as its genuine engineering judgment is the one adopted.

**The same principle, applied a fourth time.** An error variant is a claim about what happened, and a false claim in a refusal is a false claim in the audit record. This correction has now been made four times: `ToleranceError::SignatureInvalid` (Rev 1.12 — a forged grant reported as `OutOfScope`), `ResolveError::Expired` / `ReplayedNonce` (Rev 1.13, GAP-2026-08-12-001 — a replayed or late decision reported as `NeedsDapConfirmation`), and now `AdaptError::SubstitutedCorpus` / `StaleCorpus` (this gap — a poisoned or stale corpus reported as a candidate defect). The `AdaptError` enum-level doc cites all four as one principle.

**Status:** DISPOSED — the refusal vocabulary is complete (`SubstitutedCorpus`, `StaleCorpus` committed). Phase 9 (`brokkr-adapt`) can now honestly refuse a substituted or stale corpus and can be re-issued. No requirement relaxed; the ask was to *add* an honest refusal, and it was added.

— End of disposition, GAP-2026-08-19-001.
