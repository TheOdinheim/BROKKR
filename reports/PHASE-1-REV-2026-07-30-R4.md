## 1. What this revision does

Rev 1.7 corrected the barrier's egress rule — condition 1 now short-circuits on `classification ==
Public` **and** `personal.is_none()`, and condition 9 requires personal data to carry a matching BCR
tag — and added the Privileged-Context personal-data ingress rule. Those are Phase 6. This revision
places the **type surface** that makes them expressible: `PersonalDataTag` and the
`personal: Option<PersonalDataTag>` field on the flow and the custody record. **Types only.**
Scope-locked to `brokkr-core`.

## 3. `PersonalDataTag` — orthogonality is structural (Task 1)

`PersonalDataTag { purpose: Purpose, retention: RetentionPeriod }` is a **Data Classification
dimension**, composing with `Classification` and orthogonal to it — a **separate type**, never a
`Classification` variant. This is the requirement (OQGF-P-11.1), not a modelling preference: a
`Classification::Personal` variant would make "Public **and** personal" inexpressible and would
satisfy the sensitivity gate while defeating the lifecycle one. Because the tag is carried as
`Option<PersonalDataTag>` **alongside** `classification`, a datum that is `Public` and personal is
representable — and its presence triggers the lifecycle obligations at every tier, Public included.

**Derives:** `#[derive(Debug, Clone, PartialEq, Eq)]` — matching the sibling barrier structs
(`DestinationClass`, `BoundaryCustodyRecord`). Not `Copy`: `Purpose` holds a `String`. Confirmed
available (not copied): `Purpose` and `RetentionPeriod` both derive `PartialEq, Eq` (`String` and
`core::time::Duration` are both `Eq`), so the tag can too.

## 4. The field on the flow, not only the record (Task 3)

The `personal` tag is on the **flow** (both `Egress` and `Ingress`), not only on the BCR, and the doc
records why: without it, `evaluate` cannot distinguish a Public personal datum from a Public ordinary
one **when no BCR is presented** — which is exactly the case Rev 1.6's condition 1 wrongly `Allow`ed
and Rev 1.7's corrected condition 1 short-circuits on. The BCR field
(`BoundaryCustodyRecord::personal`) is what condition 9's match compares against. Both are required
fields, not `Option`-of-the-record — the *tag* is optional (a datum may not be personal), but the
*field* is present in every flow and every record.
