## 3. The three Step-0 readings the prompt required

### (a) Dependency direction — brokkr-adapt MAY depend on brokkr-sentinel

The corpus seam needs `Observation` and Gate 3 needs `screen`, both in `brokkr-sentinel`. **brokkr-adapt may depend on brokkr-sentinel.** The supporting invariant is **I-9**, whose *only* stated dependency constraint on this crate is *"`brokkr-adapt` SHALL NOT depend on `brokkr-reasoner`."* `brokkr-sentinel` is not `brokkr-reasoner`. **I-5** forbids governance crates depending on `brokkr-reasoner`/`brokkr-tools`/`brokkr-cli`; `brokkr-sentinel` is a *governance* crate, so `brokkr-adapt → brokkr-sentinel` is governance→governance, which no invariant forbids. And §6.12/§5.1 place KVASIR *consuming* HEIMDALL's screening (*"Screening is HEIMDALL's; call it, do not reimplement it"*) — the dependency is the intended design, not a workaround. I therefore did **not** consider duplicating `Observation` or reimplementing screening. Dependencies will be `brokkr-core`, `brokkr-crypto`, `brokkr-sentinel`; **never `brokkr-reasoner`** (I-9), to be confirmed with `cargo tree`.

### (b) What `generate` does — it gates, it does not invent

`generate(&SeedingIncident) -> Result<RefinedDetector, AdaptError>` returns a `RefinedDetector` whose `DetectorDelta` **cannot be derived from an incident id** — §6.12: *"KVASIR gates; it does not invent... the DetectorDelta is authored, not derived... no code turns [a seed] into detection logic. The author is a human or external tooling."* So the authored `(base, delta)` reaches `generate` as **pipeline-held state**, supplied to the `Kvasir` at construction by the human/external author — **explicitly not the reasoner** (there is no arrow from MÍMIR to KVASIR; `brokkr-adapt` SHALL NOT depend on `brokkr-reasoner`, I-9). `generate`'s job is purely Gate 1: validate the seed is genuinely confirmed (reading c), then stamp the pre-authored delta into a `RefinedDetector::new(base, delta, generation)`. It invents nothing; `UnconfirmedSeed` is its only error, as committed.

### (c) How a seed is confirmed — a seam, because the alternative is not a gate

P-6.1 requires a DAP-confirmed incident **recorded in Organ 5**, and `brokkr-adapt` SHALL NOT depend on `brokkr-audit`. So `generate` cannot itself read SAGA. The `SeedingIncident` carries its own `confirmed_by: Dap` — but **a gate that accepts its input's own claim about itself is not a gate.** To be a real gate, `generate` must verify the incident against an external source of truth through a **crate-local seam**, e.g.:

```rust
/// Whether an incident is genuinely recorded and DAP-confirmed in Organ 5.
/// Phase 11 wires this to SAGA; a test double serves Phase 9 tests.
pub trait ConfirmedIncidentLedger: Send + Sync {
    fn confirmation(&self, incident: &IncidentId) -> Option<Dap>;
}
```

`generate` refuses `UnconfirmedSeed` when `confirmation(seed.incident_id)` is `None`, **or** when the confirming `Dap` it returns differs from `seed.confirmed_by` (so the caller cannot assert a different confirmer). This makes Gate 1 a real gate at Phase 9 (checked against the seam's test double); the **production** Organ-5 verification is the seam's Phase-11 implementation. Reported honestly per the prompt: without this seam, the only available check is the caller's own `confirmed_by` — which is not a gate, and I would say so in the conformance record rather than present it as satisfied.
