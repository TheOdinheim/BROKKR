## 3. Why the build stopped (detail in the gap files)

- **GAP-2026-07-24-001 (halting).** OQGF-M-11 grants only on four conjuncts; two of them —
  *action-in-scope* (`OutOfScope`) and *action-respects-invariants* (`InvariantViolated`, the
  M-10 action-evaluation clause) — **cannot be computed from the committed types.** `Action`
  is `{ tool: ToolId, detail: String }` with no capability field; `ToolId` (REGIN vocabulary)
  and `Capability` are distinct newtypes with no committed conversion; `Invariant(String)` has
  no committed `(Action, Invariant) → bool` predicate. Building `evaluate`'s steps 3–4 would
  require inventing a convention (forbidden; binds REGIN), fail-opening (grant unchecked
  actions), or fail-closing-all (deny everything; autoimmunity). All three are DAP decisions.
- **GAP-2026-07-24-002 (secondary).** Signal 1's *intrinsic* attestation-signature check has no
  committed canonical content: `Attestation` defines no signed-content, and the only encoding
  (`canonical::write_attestation`) is private and self-including. Signal 1 has a non-inventing
  construction (resolve-to-declared-root + bind to the chain's verified current hop), so this
  gap does not halt on its own — but I do not build the workaround while GAP-…-001 halts the
  phase; I record it for joint disposition.

## 4. What I did NOT do (and why)

- **Did not create `brokkr-gate`.** Writing `evaluate` requires deciding steps 3–4; that
  decision is reserved to the architecture and the DAP (CLAUDE.md §1, §4).
- **Did not invent** a `ToolId → Capability` mapping or an invariant-evaluation predicate — the
  precise §4-forbidden move, and the one that would let Phase 4 dictate Phase 5's vocabulary.
- **Did not build a partial Signals-1–2-only gate** and call the phase done: that would be a
  fail-open gate on two M-11 conjuncts and would report M-11 as satisfied when it is not.
- **Did not relax any requirement** to close a verdict.

## 5. Design that is ready to build the moment the gaps are disposed

So disposition-to-build is fast (recorded here, not adopted): `brokkr-gate` = `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`,
deps `brokkr-core` + `brokkr-intent` + `brokkr-crypto` only (I-5/I-6 hold trivially — pure logic,
no reasoner/tools/cli, no model, no FFI). A `KeyResolver` trait (crate-owned) with one concrete
`RegistryResolver` (Option B: `SubjectId → (mldsa_pub, slhdsa_pub)` bytes, minting `DualPublicKey`
per resolve since it is not `Clone`). `Sindri<R: KeyResolver>` holds the resolver in `&self` and
implements only `evaluate`: Signal 1 (resolver + chain-binding) → Signal 2 (`verify_chain_public`,
IntentError→AnergyReason as specified, `Backend`→`ChainInvalid` fail-closed) → step 3
(`required_capability(action) ∈ current_scope()` else `OutOfScope`) → step 4 (invariant seam over
`current_invariants()` else `InvariantViolated`) → `Ok(())`. Steps 3–4 need the surface GAP-…-001
asks the DAP to place; steps 1–2 need the ruling GAP-…-002 asks for. SINDRI never mints (I-1).
