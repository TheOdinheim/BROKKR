## Correcting the framing (Task 6, stated plainly)

The prompt's context says the Phase 8.5 rebuild reported EIR's stored time as *"a test seam only"
whose decision paths take `now` per call*. **My Phase 8.5 records did not conclude that** — they
flagged EIR and HEIMDALL as **genuine I-13 violations** (`GAP-2026-08-18-001`: *"Both are genuine
I-13 violations … `resolve` and the grant-application take no `now` parameter — they read held
state"*), naming `eir.rs:232/242` and `heimdall.rs:268` exactly. The record is stated accurately here
so the history is precise (§8).

**But the deeper methodological point the prompt makes is correct and is adopted.** The Phase 8.5
*workspace grep* distinguished a `now` **struct field** from a `now` **parameter** — a matter of
**shape**, not the **property**. That distinction is necessary but not sufficient: `EIR::classify`
*takes* a `now` parameter (looks correct by shape) yet is **fed the held `st.now`** by its callers, so
a shape-matching grep cannot see that its hysteresis/chronic comparisons read a held clock. **A grep
is not a proof; the closure evidence is a table tracing every expiry/elapsed comparison to its time
source** (Task 3 below). That table — not the grep RISK-2026-0006 originally named — is the correct
closure condition, and this revision adopts it. The prior grep-based closure evidence is invalid, and
RISK-2026-0006 is updated to say so.

## Task 1 — why the build stopped

Deleting the field and giving each decision method a per-call `now` is the fix. But `resolve`,
`may_resolve`, `scan_chronic`, and `grant_heuristic` are **core trait methods**; adding `now` to any
of them changes a `brokkr-core` trait. **Compiler evidence** (change applied to `EIR::resolve`, built,
then reverted):

```
error[E0050]: method `resolve` has 3 parameters but the declaration in trait `resolve` has 2
   --> brokkr-sentinel/src/eir.rs:224:16
error: could not compile `brokkr-sentinel` (lib) due to 1 previous error
```

`ResolutionEngine::resolve` (core) declares two parameters; the impl cannot add `now` without the
trait changing. The same E0050 applies to the other three. **There is no in-scope partial fix** —
every held-clock read is in a trait method. Per the scope lock and its explicit *"if the break is in
another crate, STOP and report rather than reaching outside scope,"* and per CLAUDE.md §4 (committed
types cannot support the task without editing a protected crate → STOP, never pick the reading that
proceeds), I stopped and filed **GAP-2026-08-18-002**. The experiment is reverted; `brokkr-sentinel`
is unchanged and clean.

**Why SINDRI and BIFRÖST did not hit this:** their core traits (`CostimulationGate`,
`ContextClearance`) were revised to add `now` by the core I-13 revision `7d47d2a` **before** those
crate revisions. That revision touched `gate.rs` and `reasoner.rs` only — **not** `resolution.rs` or
`tolerance.rs`. The core prerequisite for the sentinel fix was never placed; that is the gap.

## Task 2 — two-verdict tests

**Not written** — the build stopped before any source change. When the core prerequisite lands and
this revision is re-issued, the tests are: EIR decision-expiry across its expiry (one instance,
`resolve` twice — success then `Err(Expired)`; **nonce handling is the subtle part**: a decision that
resolves once is recorded as accepted and cannot resolve again, so the expiry case must use a
**separate** decision with a fresh nonce, or the second `resolve` would fail as `ReplayedNonce`, not
`Expired` — the test must isolate expiry from replay); HEIMDALL grant-expiry across its expiry (the
**fails-toward-blindness** load-bearing case — an unexpiring grant leaves a detector suppressed
indefinitely); EIR hysteresis (`may_resolve` NotYet before dwell/hold, Eligible after — proving the
clock ages). Recorded here so the re-issue is scoped, not exploratory.
