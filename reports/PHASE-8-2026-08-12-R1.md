## 2. The three Step-0 readings the prompt required — resolved and reported

**(a) EIR hysteresis needs elapsed time; `EscalationType` carries `Duration`, `Timestamp` is epoch
millis.** EIR tracks per-escalation state: `raised_at: Timestamp`, `clear_since: Option<Timestamp>` (when
the clear condition began *continuously* holding), and `active`. The trait's `may_resolve(&self,
&EscalationId)` takes no `now`, so **`now` is injected into EIR's state** (a `set_now`; the orchestrator's
clock at Phase 11) — never a wall-clock read, keeping the gate deterministically testable. Elapsed and
held spans are computed in **milliseconds** (`now.0 - raised_at.0`, saturating) and widened to `u128` to
compare against `Duration::as_millis()`. So EIR knows "how long up" = `now − raised_at` and "how long
held" = `now − clear_since`.

**(b) The host-harm denominator is "governed actions evaluated in the window".** The monitor **observes;
it does not reach** (I-5). HEIMDALL holds a `governed: u64` counter incremented via
`record_governed_action()` and a `Vec<HostHarmIncident>` fed via `record_incident()` — both **supplied by
the orchestrator** (Phase 11), exactly as observations and detectors are fed. HEIMDALL never calls into
`brokkr-gate` or `brokkr-barrier` to count; it counts what it is shown.

**(c) `ResolutionDecision` carries a `DualSignature` — what it covers and how EIR verifies it.** §6.8
specifies no encoding, so this phase **defines one** (Task-2 discipline, tag `brokkr-sentinel:resolution:v1`):
the signature covers the decision's `escalation`, `cleared_condition`, `dap`, and `at` (everything except
the signature). **Verification is in scope and load-bearing:** `resolve` verifies the signature under the
declared DAP public key, and a decision that does not verify is **not DAP-confirmed** → `NeedsDapConfirmation`.
This is precisely how the OQGF-P-8.5 asymmetry is enforced — a `ResolutionDecision` always carries a `Dap`
field (required), so "without DAP" can only mean "without a valid DAP *signature*," and the signature is
what makes the confirmation real. Defining the encoding is not inventing governance (Task-2 discipline,
explicitly authorized by §6.8's silence).

## 3. Two further design readings worth recording

- **"Sustained" is not numerically defined in the placed text.** §6.7 and AMD-002 OQGF-P-5a say
  autoimmunity is a *"sustained rise in host-harm rate above the declared bound"* but give no number of
  windows or duration. Rather than choose a threshold silently, HEIMDALL takes a **DAP-declared
  `sustained_threshold`, injected at construction** — the same posture as `bound` and `blast_radius`. The
  monitor tracks consecutive over-bound windows (`roll_window`) and flags autoimmunity when the injected
  threshold is reached. A single over-bound window is **not** autoimmunity (tested).
- **Zero denominator reports `NaN`, never `0.0`.** A window that evaluated no governed actions has no
  evidence; a rate of `0.0` would claim safety from none. `window_rate` returns `f64::NAN` when the
  denominator is zero (f64 division is total — no panic), and autoimmunity is `false` (NaN is not a
  breach). Tested by `test_host_harm_zero_denominator`.
- **`ToleranceError` has no signature-failure variant — flagged, not a STOP.** Task 4 requires verifying
  the grant signature, but `ToleranceError` is `{NonSuppressibleGate, FailsCentralTolerance, Expired,
  OutOfScope}` — none names "bad signature." A forged grant **must** be refused (fail-closed), so
  `grant_heuristic` refuses an unauthentically-signed grant as `OutOfScope` (a grant not validly signed by
  the DAP carries no authorized scope). This is a **naming imprecision**, not a security gap or an invented
  rule — every reading refuses — so it is a candidate for a future additive `ToleranceError::SignatureInvalid`,
  **flagged here for the DAP** rather than filed as a §4 gap (nothing is unbuildable and no governance is
  invented). `screen` for an unregistered detector id similarly fails closed as `FailsCentralTolerance` —
  no certification is issued without an actual clean run over a verified corpus.

## 4. What was built

- **`observation.rs`** — `Observation{Crossing, Authorization, Signalled, Hop}`, the `Detector` trait
  (`id` + `observe`), `DetectionVerdict{Clear, Fired}`, and the `SelfSetCorpus` seam. **A detector cannot
  grant anything (structural):** `DetectionVerdict` has exactly two variants and no action-permitting one —
  the strongest act is to *fire* (Task 1, confirmed).
- **`canonical.rs`** — four encodings (`corpus_signed_content`, `grant_signed_content`,
  `resolution_signed_content`, `signal_signed_content`), `Canon` discipline, exhaustive enum tags (no
  catch-all), tags `brokkr-sentinel:{corpus,tolerance-grant,resolution,signal}:v1`.
- **`heimdall.rs`** — `Heimdall`: `ToleranceController` (`grant_heuristic`, `screen`, `host_harm`) + the
  host-harm monitor (`record_governed_action`, `record_incident`, `roll_window`, `assess_response` →
  `StormAssessment{Within, Exceeds, Unenforceable}`), reconciliation (`reconcile`), and raise-only Signal
  emission (signed under HEIMDALL's own key; `PostureEffect` has only `Raise`).
- **`eir.rs`** — `Eir`: `ResolutionEngine` (`may_resolve`, `resolve`, `scan_chronic`) with injected `now`,
  the fail-safe asymmetry, and incident preservation by construction (EIR holds no records/detectors).

**Screening's digest check is load-bearing (Task 3):** `screen` recomputes the digest over
`corpus.observations()` and compares it to the declared `SelfSet.corpus_digest` — it does **not** trust the
corpus's self-reported `digest()`. A mismatch is `FailsCentralTolerance` and **nothing runs**, closing the
substituted-corpus attack (proven: the spy detector is never observed).

**Incident preservation (OQGF-P-8.4):** EIR holds only posture state (which escalations are up, since when,
whether their clear condition holds). It holds **no** incident records and **no** learned detectors, so
`resolve` marking an escalation inactive erases nothing — SAGA (Phase 7) and KVASIR (Phase 9) retain their
records independently, and EIR depends on neither (I-5). The response stands down; the intelligence does
not, because EIR never held it.
