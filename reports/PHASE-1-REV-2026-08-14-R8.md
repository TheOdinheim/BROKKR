## 2. What was placed (Task 1)

Two variants added to `ResolveError`, in the existing message style (`… (OQGF-P-8.5)`), after
`NeedsDapConfirmation` (grouping the three P-8.5 refusals):

- **`Expired`** — *"the resolution decision has expired (OQGF-P-8.5)"*; per-variant doc: `now >
  decision.expiry`, validly made and signed, arrived too late — operational latency, not an attack.
- **`ReplayedNonce`** — *"the resolution decision's nonce was already accepted (OQGF-P-8.5)"*; per-variant
  doc: the decision is **authentic** — correctly signed, by the right DAP, over criteria that really were
  met once — now presented again.

An **enum-level doc** mirrors `ToleranceError`'s Rev 1.12 convention and records the two rationales §6.8
requires: **two variants, not one `Stale`**, because an expiry is latency and a replay is an attack, and
collapsing them files both under one word; and **neither reuses `NeedsDapConfirmation`**, because a replayed
decision *is* DAP-confirmed, so reporting missing confirmation during a replay points the investigation at
the one thing that is not wrong. The two enums (`ResolveError`, `ToleranceError`) are now deliberate mirrors
— each carries a "a refusal names what it actually is" doc and its own attack-vs-benign variant.
