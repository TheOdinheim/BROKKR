## 2. What was placed (types only — no logic)

Two types in `tolerance.rs`, plus one import and one re-export. **No detection logic, no screening, no rate
computation** — those are Phase 8.

- **`DefensiveResponse`** (Task 1) — a `Copy` enum over the four §6.7-named responses that can constitute
  host harm: `Anergy`, `Deny`, `Quarantine`, `Throttle`. Documented as the **kind** of harm, not a verdict:
  the deterministic verdict types (`AnergyReason`, `BarrierVerdict`) stay where they are; `DefensiveResponse`
  is the after-the-fact classification a confirmed incident falls into, and does not replace or convert
  either.
- **`HostHarmIncident`** (Task 2) — exactly the §6.7 shape
  `{ response: DefensiveResponse, action: Action, confirmed_by: Dap, at: Timestamp }`, documented as the
  deliberate mirror of `SeedingIncident`: `SeedingIncident` is a DAP-confirmed **true** positive (the only
  seed for learning, OQGF-P-6.1); `HostHarmIncident` is a DAP-confirmed **false** positive (the only thing
  that counts toward the host-harm rate, OQGF-P-1). Confirmation is a human act in both directions —
  whether a blocked action was legitimate is not derivable from the action. `confirmed_by` is **required,
  not `Option`**: an unconfirmed report is not a host-harm incident and this type cannot represent one. The
  doc records that the **rate** is computed in `brokkr-sentinel` (Phase 8), not here, and that §13 records
  it as a **lower bound** (unreported false positives never appear, biasing the measured rate toward
  looking safer than it is).

Import added: `use crate::gate::Action;` (intra-crate; no cyclic-crate issue — `gate` is a sibling module).
Re-export (Task 4): `DefensiveResponse` and `HostHarmIncident` added to the `tolerance::{…}` line,
alphabetically, matching the existing convention.
